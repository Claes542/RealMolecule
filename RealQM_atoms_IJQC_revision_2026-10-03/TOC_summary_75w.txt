RealQM models an atom as non-overlapping unit charge densities meeting at
free interfaces, with the nuclear charge as the only input. Two filled
packings occur and no third: the antipodal pair and the octet of two dual
tetrahedra. Row capacities of the periodic table are sums of these alone,
and a period ends when its capacity is exhausted. Energies follow to a few
per cent from hydrogen to krypton, with nothing fitted.
