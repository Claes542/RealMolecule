      Cosmology 3D on GPU. N = 200 cubic grid (8 000 000 cells). Compressible
      Euler with self-gravity Poisson Δφ + G·ρ = 0 (red-black Gauss-Seidel,
      settled at start then a few sweeps per fluid step), body force ρ∇φ,
      ideal-gas pressure p = γe with e = |ρ|T.
      Initial state: a signed ±mass fluctuation of zero mean (~50/50 by
      construction, correlation length set by the "web scale" slider), cold at
      T₀ = 0.01, at rest.
      Route A — inertia is positive for both signs, u = m/(|ρ|+ρ_min),
      so the sign of ρ enters only the gravitational coupling; like mass attracts,
      unlike repels, and the two phases are exact mirrors.
      Transport is upwind (donor-cell) and the continuity equation is
      diffusion-free: it carries the signed charge with flux ρu and
      nothing else.
      What sets the web scale is the Jeans length
      λ_J = 2π√(γT₀)/√(G|ρ|) — a physical scale, not a tuning knob — and
      what decides whether the web stays fine or smooths out is the adiabatic index
      γ_ad = 1+γ: below 4/3 the Jeans mass falls under compression and
      structure keeps fragmenting (fine persistent web); above 4/3 it rises and collapse
      arrests early (coarse). The shock-viscosity heating term
      ν += C·h·|∇·u| is retained but off by default: offline tests
      showed it does not arrest collapse — it drives a heating runaway that erases the
      web, and it gets worse, not better, under refinement.
      Outer-box BC frozen; φ Dirichlet-zero.
