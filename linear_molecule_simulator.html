Linear Molecule Simulator — axisym
2D (z,ρ) RealQM solver, exact for linear molecules.
Protocol: geometry from the sharp interface (R scan, h ladder → h→0); binding energy from the weak (mol_fast) interface at h = 0.1 exactly, at the observed R and at the sharp R. rc of a pseudo-atom is fitted to the atom's IE sum, per interface.
Runs are made from the command line and land in axisym_runs/; this page discovers them. ?mol= selects one. Density views: axisym_view.html.
